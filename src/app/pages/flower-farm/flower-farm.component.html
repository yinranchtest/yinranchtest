VENTURE TO THE COUNTRYSIDE

Experience Simple Country Luxury when visiting Yin Ranch for a Day Visit! We offer Brunch in the Flower Farm Pavilion, a Seasonal You Pick Flower Farm, Garden Tours, Workshops to connect with nature and our beautiful Yolo Country countryside.
VIEW EVENT CALENTER

BRUNCH

Join us on the weekends for Brunch in our Flower Farm Pavilion most Saturdays & Sundays — 10:00am to 3:00pm with the kitchen closing at 2:30pm.

Begin your Day Visit with a delicious meal and enjoy seasonal selections from our al-la-carte Brunch menu.

PICK YOUR OWN BOUQUET

Our Seasonal Flower Field is Now Open!

Visit our seasonal flower farm for this exclusive experience. Enjoy the open space to connect with the flowers, and take home a beautiful hand picked bouquet. Our field is open seasonally May - October on most Saturdays and Sundays — 8:30am to 2:30pm.

dried floral experience

The Dried Floral Experience will return Fall 2025

Pick and arrange your own dried flower bouquet in our dreamy Flower Barn. We’ve been preserving our favorite spring and summer blooms to offer this unique artisanal experience you can’t find anywhere else!

GARDEN TOURS

Garden Tours every 2nd & 4th Saturday’s of each month!

Join us for a Seasonal guided tours and enjoy the maturity of 150 year old trees alongside our lovingly manicured gardens. We have thousands of perennials throughout our grounds so you’ll get a different experience each season.

CREATIVE CLASSES

Join us for a Gather & Create Workshop in our Flower Barn! From making wreaths and pumpkulents, to learning how to create seasonal arrangements, you’ll bring out your inner creative and take home a beautiful creation!

THE MARKETPLACE

Saturdays & Sundays 10am - 1pm

For your kitchen, pantry and bath, the Marketplace has house-made and thoughtfully selected items from local farms and makers. Visit our flower barn with everlasting dried flowers or seasonal fresh flowers ready for your table.
