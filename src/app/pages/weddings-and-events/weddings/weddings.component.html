WEDDINGS

Your Dream Wedding

Nestled amidst the verdant farmlands of Northern California, yin Ranch is a five-star, historic wedding venue and country estate designed to bring a fairytale wedding to life. Wander through formal gardens, past an elegant historical Inn and chic event barn into a world of natural beauty. Surrounded by panoramic views of the Blue Hills, Park Winters embodies weddings of simple country luxury—with touches of sparkling splendor.

 

Yin Ranch offers customized options to meet the wishes of each couple. Depending upon the season, Yin Ranch can accommodate up to 150-200 guests. Host a rehearsal dinner, the wedding of your dreams and a farewell brunch—we love weddings and are honored to assist in making your dreams come true.
