INDULGE IN SIMPLE COUNTRY LUXURY

The Inn at Yin Ranch celebrates luxury with exquisitely appointed rooms, linens and robes, an elegant pool, a library and conversation salons, and a seasonally inspired breakfast. Savor the views overlooking our historic grounds, beautifully manicured gardens and surrounding landscape.

SPECIAL TOUCHES

Bright, natural light and subtle colors infuse each space with an elegant ambiance. Each room is furnished with antiques found around the world—all with views of our serene gardens.

You'll find luxurious Egyptian cotton linens on our beds, opulent bathrooms, Frette robes and organic bath and body products. All rooms also have premium satellite channels.

POOL AND HOT TUB

Slip into our beach entry pool to melt away the day or enjoy a contemplative soak in our relaxing hot tub. We have a state-of-the-art sound system to customize your music choices for an evening with friends as you watch the stars appear.

FIRE FEATURE

Overlooking the distant mountains and fields is our majestic 24-foot fire feature. The perfect spot to visit with another or read a book from our library while savoring a bubbly, a glass of wine or local craft beer.

BREAKFAST FOR TWO

Sourced from the region’s finest ingredients, breakfast at the Inn is seasonally inspired by farms nearby. Our own kitchen prepares it to perfection with fresh brewed coffee and a selection of fine teas.

OUR GARDENS AND FARM

Shaded walkways, a lovely fountain in the distance, lavender and rose gardens to view—Yin Ranch is an invitation to melt away the everyday.

The Yin Ranch Flower Farm is nestled in the midst of fields, orchards and lush farmlands and is open for day visits. From our flower field and barn to our sunset lounge to linger—The Yin Ranch Flower Farm weaves memories into each view and every moment.
