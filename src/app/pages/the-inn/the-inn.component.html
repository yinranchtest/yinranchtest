ESCAPE TO OUR COUNTRY OASIS

Enjoy a stay at our romantic boutique bed and breakfast in Northern California. A lovingly restored 1865 historic farm house, the Inn features four elegantly appointed guest suites, a dining room, social parlors, pool and hot tub. The Inn at Yin Ranch is ideal for any special getaway, anniversary or babymoon.
